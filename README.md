CALA VERDE: open-world crime in a golden-hour coastal city. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's Make GTA 6 bounty, $1,666 in USDC. An original 3D open world: steal cars, run eight story jobs with your crew Rico and Mara, outrun the 5-star CVPD dispatch with roadblocks and the CONDOR-2 helicopter, buy faster cars in the garage, and pull the Verde Vault heist as the sun goes down. Three synth radio stations, a live minimap, and saves in your browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network at runtime (three.js r180 is vendored under `game/vendor/`).

## Controls

WASD move / drive · Shift run · E enter/exit car · F or click fire · 1/2/3 weapons · Q crew ability · R radio · P pause · M mute.

## Missions

1. First Light — meet Rico in the plaza. 2. Borrowed Wheels — steal a car, deliver it to the garage. 3. Parcel Run — bank steps to the hideout. 4. Street Heat — 5-checkpoint race. 5. Loud Message — clear the docks. 6. Golden Hour — drive Mara to the marina. 7. Smoke Screen — escape the CVPD to the hideout. 8. The Verde Vault — the final heist.
